# The native-memory fix: windows-dev, 2026-10-20

Machine: see bench/baseline/MACHINE.md. Native library built from branch perf/native-memory with GraalVM Community
Edition 25.0.2 (the toolchain of the release wheels). Python 3.12.7, pyarrow 26.0.0, DuckDB 1.5.6, NetworkX from
PyPI on the same day. The design is in docs/designs/engineering-review-native-memory.md and decisions D247 to D252.

## Graph: python/benchmarks/compare.py, 1,000,000 edges over 200,000 nodes, 500,000 deletes, 200 queries

    NODUSDB_LIBRARY=target/native/libnodusdb.dll python python/benchmarks/compare.py

The cross-check passed first: 50 k-hop and 50 common-neighbor queries agree across 7 configurations.

| Configuration | Insert/s | Delete/s | 2-hop median | 3-hop median | Common neighbors median |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| NodusDB, single-edge calls | 163,443 | 168,178 | 9.6 us | 11.7 us | 8.3 us |
| NodusDB, batch of tuples | 477,112 | 469,098 | 9.5 us | 11.3 us | 8.3 us |
| NodusDB, flat array('q') | 810,031 | 807,466 | 9.4 us | 11.5 us | 8.3 us |
| NetworkX, single | 176,695 | 405,820 | 11.9 us | 25.3 us | 5.0 us |
| NetworkX, batch | 207,937 | 393,485 | 12.4 us | 26.2 us | 5.1 us |
| SQLite, single | 147,211 | 141,748 | 39.6 us | 78.5 us | 15.9 us |
| SQLite, batch | 187,884 | 175,200 | 43.7 us | 80.9 us | 16.5 us |

Against the 0.1.0 library (release-windows-dev.txt): flat inserts 162,689 -> 810,031, flat deletes 137,975 ->
807,466, 3-hop median 27.3 -> 11.5 us, common neighbors 15.6 -> 8.3 us. The build at commit 0b05475 (before
native memory) measured 1.06 to 1.30 million inserts a second and a 10.3 to 10.7 us 3-hop median, so the 3-hop
median is back and inserts recovered five of the six to eight times they lost.

## Native against JVM: python/benchmarks/native_ratio.py, two runs

| Probe | Native | JVM | Ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| add_edge | 1,004 to 1,008 ns/op | 375 to 410 ns/op | 2.46 to 2.68 |
| has_edge | 68 to 71 ns/op | 33 to 39 ns/op | 1.75 to 2.15 |
| lake ingest, 250,000 rows | 424 to 440 ns/row | 182 to 199 ns/row | 2.22 to 2.33 |
| lake sum | 1.6 to 1.7 ns/row | 1.4 ns/row | 1.15 to 1.18 |
| lake flush, Snappy | 869 to 904 ns/row | 374 to 378 ns/row | 2.30 to 2.42 |

The 0.1.0 library measured about 9 for add_edge and 16 for has_edge on this laptop, and 5.8 to 7.7 and 12.5 to
14.2 in CI.

## Lake suite: benchmark_lake_heavyweights.py, NodusDB engines only

    python python/benchmarks/benchmark_lake_heavyweights.py run \
        --engines nodus-lake,nodus-lake-batch,nodus-lake-uncompressed \
        --out bench/baseline/lake-heavyweights-native-memory-windows-dev.json

The other engines were not re-run; their figures for the same day, machine and package versions are in
lake-release-windows-dev.txt. The "did not complete" lines in the output are engine and workload pairs the suite
does not define.

File sizes are unchanged (3.95 MB for the Snappy flush, 5.40 MB uncompressed) and every source returns the revenue
total 27,486,629.10.

Against the other engines on the same day: ingest 994,900 rows/s against PyArrow 547,340, SQLite 285,608 and
DuckDB 147,314; columnar upserts 2,002,694/s against PyArrow's dict 329,166 and SQLite 243,167; the in-memory sum
2.6 ms against DuckDB's 7.9 ms over its own files. PyArrow still flushes 250,000 rows with Snappy faster (0.194 s
against 0.231 s), and deletes and single-call upserts through Python stay slower than PyArrow and SQLite because
each one is a separate foreign call.
